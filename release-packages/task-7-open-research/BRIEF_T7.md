# Task 7: Open Research Task and Oral Walkthrough

Worth 35% of the module assignment (20% + 15%) | Individual work | Module code PRG1

## What you're doing

This is the final and largest task in the assignment, worth 35% of the total mark, made up of two parts: a short, open problem that you research and solve independently (20%), and a short oral walkthrough of your solution (15%).

Unlike every other task in this assignment, you are not given a starter file, a written specification, or a test suite. You are given a problem statement of one or two sentences. Working out what it actually requires, what to assume, and how to check your own solution is genuinely part of the task.

## The problem

> Write a program that tells the user the current time in a different city, given their local time and the two locations.

That is the whole brief. You choose the programming language. You decide how the user provides the two locations and their local time, whether to account for things like daylight saving time, and how to structure the program. Reasonable, well-explained assumptions are expected and will not be penalised; unreasonable or unexplained ones will be.

## What to submit

- Your working code, in whatever language you have chosen.
- A short written note (half a page is plenty) covering: what you understood the problem to require, any assumptions you made and why, what you researched and where (including any AI tool use: what you asked, and what you did with the answer), and anything you would do differently with more time. This task is the one most likely to involve genuine independent research, including AI tools; being able to show your own reasoning alongside that research is exactly what this task is assessing, and it is part of how the written note is marked.
- This is separate from the end-of-module AI use reflection, `PRG1_AI_Use_Reflection`, which covers the whole assignment, is mandatory to submit, and is not marked. Complete both; the written note above covers this task specifically.

Write your code and your written note in this task's folder; use `ANSWERS_T7.md` for the written note.

## The oral walkthrough

Shortly after submission, you will have a short one-to-one conversation with a member of the teaching team about your solution. This is not a second test of the same material with a different mark scheme attached; it is a chance to demonstrate, in your own words, that you understand what you built and why.

This takes place remotely, on the final day of the module, which is already a remote delivery day. If you cannot make a slot that day, it will be rescheduled for shortly afterwards rather than moved in person. The conversation is recorded; this is so the marker can revisit your responses if your grade is borderline, and so a moderator can review it afterwards, not for any other purpose.

You should be ready to:

- Explain your code, section by section, in your own words.
- Justify specific design decisions: why you structured it the way you did, why you chose the assumptions you chose.
- Respond to a small change or an unfamiliar scenario put to you on the spot, for example being asked how your program would need to change to handle a new requirement.

You do not need to prepare a presentation. You do need to genuinely understand your own submission well enough to talk through it without notes.

## A note on approach

This task deliberately does not tell you which session's constructs to use. Use whatever combination of variables, functions, conditionals, loops, strings, lists, dictionaries, files, or classes genuinely fits the problem you have scoped for yourself. There is no requirement to use every construct covered in the module, and no bonus for using more of them than the problem actually needs.

## If you used an AI tool

See "What to submit" above: AI tool use on this task is covered in your written note, not in a separate log.

## Submission

Submit your code and `ANSWERS_T7.md`, with any AI tool use covered within it as described above. You will also complete the separate, whole-assignment `PRG1_AI_Use_Reflection` once, at the end of the module. The sign-up details for your oral walkthrough slot will be communicated separately by your teaching team.
